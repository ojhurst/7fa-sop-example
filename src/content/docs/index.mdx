## What this is

A starter kit for the internal wiki every seven-figure agency eventually wishes it had built sooner. 80 SOP stubs across paid, organic, sales, onboarding, operations, measurement, creative, retention, and AI automation. Fork it, rename it, start filling in your version of the truth.

## How to use it

1. Clone this repo.
2. Rename it to something that means something to your team.
3. Open any stub, delete the TODO, write down how you actually do it.
4. Push. Teach your team to look here first.

## Bonus — Command Center dashboard

A starter [Command Center dashboard](/command-center) ships with the repo. One screen with placeholders for your team, vendors, deadlines, KPIs, and SOP shortcuts. Fork the repo, replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]`, and you have a single page that the whole team opens every morning.

## The rule

If a question gets asked twice, it becomes a page here. No exceptions.
